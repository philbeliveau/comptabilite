Add to ThemeQCExtension.js:

1. **`REPORT_INTROS` dictionary** — a JS object keyed by URL path fragment, with values containing French pedagogical text. Include entries for ALL reports (native + extensions):

   Native Fava reports (match by URL path):
   - `"income_statement"`: Titre "Etat des resultats (revenus et depenses)". Explain: shows all revenue earned and expenses incurred over a period. Used by the consultant to understand profitability and by the CPA to prepare the T2. Source: Fava built-in (Beancount Revenus/Depenses accounts).
   - `"balance_sheet"`: Titre "Bilan". Explain: snapshot of what the company owns (assets), owes (liabilities), and net worth (equity) at a given date. Used by the CPA for the T2 balance sheet. Source: Fava built-in.
   - `"trial_balance"`: Titre "Balance de verification". Explain: list of all accounts with their debit/credit balances. If total debits = total credits, the books are balanced. The CPA uses this as the primary input for the tax return. Source: Fava built-in.
   - `"journal"`: Titre "Journal general". Explain: chronological log of every transaction in the books. Useful for tracing how a specific entry was recorded. Source: Fava built-in.

   Extension reports (match by `extension/ClassName`):
   - `"extension/ApprobationExtension"`: File d'approbation. Explain: transactions categorized by AI that need human review before being committed to the official books. Approving moves them from pending to the ledger.
   - `"extension/PaieQCExtension"`: Tableau de bord de la paie. Explain: year-to-date payroll summary showing gross salary, all employee deductions (QPP, RQAP, AE, impots), employer contributions (FSS, CNESST, normes du travail), and net pay.
   - `"extension/TaxesQCExtension"`: Suivi TPS/TVQ. Explain: tracks the GST (5%) and QST (9.975%) collected from clients and paid on expenses. The net amount is what you must remit to the government.
   - `"extension/DpaQCExtension"`: Amortissement (DPA/CCA). Explain: tracks capital assets (computers, furniture, etc.) and their annual depreciation deduction. The CPA uses this for the CCA schedule on the tax return.
   - `"extension/PretActionnaireExtension"`: Pret actionnaire. Explain: tracks money flowing between you personally and your corporation. Important: if the corp lends you money and it is not repaid within 1 year of the fiscal year-end, it becomes taxable income (s.15(2)).
   - `"extension/ExportCPAExtension"`: Export pour le comptable. Explain: generates the complete year-end package (trial balance, financial statements, schedules) that your CPA needs to prepare tax returns.
   - `"extension/EcheancesExtension"`: Echeances fiscales. Explain: calendar of upcoming tax deadlines (GST/QST remittance, T4/RL-1, T2/CO-17) with countdown alerts.
   - `"extension/RecusExtension"`: Recus et factures. Explain: upload receipts and invoices to match them to bank transactions for audit trail and expense justification.

   All text MUST be:
   - In French
   - Beginner-level (no unexplained jargon)
   - 2-3 sentences for `explication`
   - Include `qui` (who uses it) and `fonction` (source Python module/function)
